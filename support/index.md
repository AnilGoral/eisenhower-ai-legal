# Eisenhower AI – Support

## Contact

Email **goralanil@gmail.com**. Eisenhower AI is made and run by
Anil Goral.

It helps if you tell us your iPhone or iPad model, your iOS version, the app
version (shown on the App Store page or in iPhone Settings → General → iPhone
Storage → Eisenhower AI) and what you were doing when the problem happened.
Please do not send us your task list unless we ask for a specific part of it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where are my tasks stored?**
Only on your device, as Markdown files in the app's folder. You can see them in
the Files app: On My iPhone (or On My iPad) → Eisenhower AI → Eisenhower AI Next.
We never receive a copy.

**Do my tasks sync between devices?**
No. There is no cloud sync. Each device has its own task list.

**How do I back up my tasks or move them to a new device?**
The app's folder is included in your normal iCloud or computer backup of the
device, so restoring that backup on a new device brings your tasks with it. You
can also copy the folder yourself in the Files app, or share a single task from
the app as a Markdown file.

**Do I need AI Capture?**
No. Without it, the app still splits what you write into tasks on your
device and leaves them unsorted for you to place, sending nothing anywhere
("No AI" in Settings → AI). AI Capture is an optional, paid extra.

**What does AI Capture send, and is it stored?**
The text you type into AI Capture, your device's language/region setting and
time zone, a random request number and proof from Apple that the request comes
from the genuine app, plus your sign-in token so your subscription can be
checked. It goes through Cloudflare to an AI model that runs on our own
computer. We do not store the text or the answer, and we do not use it to train
models. You can turn AI Capture off in Settings → AI → Withdraw consent. See the
Privacy Policy for details.

**Why does AI Capture say it is unavailable?**
The most common reasons:

- our AI computer is off, asleep, busy or being maintained. AI Capture runs on a
  computer we operate ourselves, not in a data centre, so it is sometimes
  unavailable, occasionally for hours;
- you are not signed in, or your subscription has ended;
- you have used this period's allowance;
- AI Capture is not offered in your country;
- your device is offline.

Try again later. In the meantime your capture still becomes tasks, unsorted,
for you to place.

**How many AI Captures does the subscription include?**
Up to 500 requests in each 30-day period. The count resets when you subscribe
and at each renewal. A request that is turned away before the AI starts working
on it is not counted. One request can contain up to 8,000 characters.

**I reinstalled the app or changed device. How do I get my subscription back?**
Sign in with the same Apple ID on the account screen (Settings → AI → Account
and subscription), then tap "Restore purchases".

**Why does Sign in with Apple ask for my email address?**
Our account service needs an email address to create your account, and it is
how your iPhone and the Eisenhower AI desktop app end up on the same account:
if you sign in on the desktop with the same confirmed email address, both share
one subscription. You can choose "Hide My Email" when Apple asks. We never ask
for your name and do not use the address for marketing.

**Which computers are signed in to my account? Can I sign them out?**
If you also use the desktop app, the account screen lists the computers signed
in to your account under "Computers signed in", with when each was added and
last used. If you do not recognise one, tap "Sign out all computers", then sign
in again on the ones you use.

**What happens when I sign out?**
The app ends your session on our account service, removes your sign-in from this
device and stops linking purchases on this device to your account. Your tasks
stay where they are, and your subscription keeps running.

**Reminders show my task titles on the lock screen. Can I hide them?**
Yes. Reminders are notifications on your device. In iPhone Settings →
Notifications → Eisenhower AI → Show Previews, choose "When Unlocked" or
"Never".

**Do you use analytics, ads or tracking?**
No. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software.

## How to delete your account

You only have an account if you signed in to use the AI subscription.

1. If you have a subscription, cancel it first (see below). Deleting your
   account does **not** cancel it, and Apple keeps charging until you do. The
   deletion screen has a "Manage App Store subscriptions" link for this.
2. In the app, open Settings → AI → Account and subscription.
3. Tap "Delete account", read the notice, then tap "Delete account
   permanently".
4. Apple asks you to confirm with Sign in with Apple. If you close Apple's sheet,
   nothing is deleted.

Deleting your account:

- signs out every computer connected to it;
- deletes your plan, subscription end date, usage counter and computer keys.
  If your plan is paid and you have used AI Capture in the current 30-day
  period, the number of requests used is kept, under the account's random
  identifier and without your email address, until that period ends. If you
  restore the subscription on another account in the meantime, it carries
  over, so deleting an account does not reset the monthly allowance;
- removes the app from Sign in with Apple in your Apple Account. If the app says
  this could not be done, remove it yourself: iPhone Settings → your name →
  Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple → Eisenhower AI → Stop Using;
- deletes your account itself: your user identifier, Apple identifier, email
  address and sessions;
- signs you out on this device.

It does not delete:

- **your App Store subscription** (see above);
- **your customer record at RevenueCat**, our subscription service, which holds
  your old account identifier and purchase history. It is not deleted
  automatically: email goralanil@gmail.com and we will delete it;
- **Apple's own purchase records**;
- **the tasks on your device.** To remove those, delete the app.

If you can no longer open the app, email goralanil@gmail.com and we will tell you how
we can confirm the account is yours without asking for more data than needed.
Writing from the email address you use with Sign in with Apple helps.

## How to cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so they are cancelled in your Apple ID
settings, not in the app:

1. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings and tap your name.
2. Tap Subscriptions.
3. Choose Eisenhower AI and tap Cancel Subscription.

You can also go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions, tap "Manage
subscription" on the app's account screen, or open the App Store, tap your
profile picture and choose Subscriptions. On a Mac, use App Store → your name →
Account Settings → Subscriptions.

After you cancel, you keep AI access until the end of the period you have
already paid for, and the subscription does not renew. To be sure a renewal is
not charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

**Refunds** are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If
Apple grants a refund, AI access for that subscription ends straight away.
